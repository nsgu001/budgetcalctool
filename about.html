About BudgetCalcTool.org

Independent personal finance tools built by real people who've been there.

Our Story

BudgetCalcTool.org was born from a simple frustration: most budget calculators online are either too simplistic to be useful or buried under layers of ads and upsells. We wanted something different. Something honest. Something that actually helps.

Started in 2024 by Marcus Thompson, a former financial counselor who spent a decade helping families dig out of debt and build real budgets that stick. The tools here are the same ones he used in counseling sessions, refined and made accessible for everyone.

Our Approach

We believe budgeting shouldn't require a finance degree. Every tool on this site is designed to be:

  • Actually useful: Real calculations, not gimmicks
  • Free: No paywalls, no "premium features"
  • Private: Your data stays in your browser (localStorage only)
  • Honest: Clear disclaimers about what we can and can't do
